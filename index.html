What We Offer
Comprehensive Physiotherapy Services

From acute injuries to chronic conditions, our expert therapists provide personalised treatment plans to get you back to your best.

🔍
Initial Assessment
Thorough evaluation of your condition, medical history, and movement analysis to create your personalised treatment plan.
$120
🤲
Manual Therapy
Hands-on techniques including joint mobilisation, soft tissue massage, and manipulation to relieve pain and restore movement.
$95
🏃
Exercise Therapy
Customised exercise programs to strengthen muscles, improve flexibility, and prevent future injuries.
$85
⚡
Electrotherapy
Advanced electrical stimulation treatments including TENS, IFT, and EMS to reduce pain and accelerate healing.
$70
🔊
Ultrasound Therapy
Deep tissue ultrasound treatment to reduce inflammation, break down scar tissue, and promote healing.
$60
🏠
Home Visit
Physiotherapy at your home for patients who are unable to travel. Full treatment in the comfort of your home.
$180
